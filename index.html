Sustainability Commitments
Small steps, but we mean it
♻️

Recyclable Packaging

Our packaging uses biodegradable poly bags. The QR hang tag is printed on recycled card stock — no plastic tags.

🌾

Natural Fiber First

80% of the shirt is natural cotton — breathable, biodegradable, and gentler on the environment than full synthetics.

🤝

Local Sourcing

We plan to source from Philippine-based suppliers to reduce shipping emissions and support local workers and SMEs.

📦

Low Overproduction

We use a pre-order and batch model to reduce unsold stock. Less waste, more intentional production per demand.

Note from the team: We're students — we can't promise a perfect supply chain on day one. But sustainability is part of our business plan and something we genuinely want to build into Solaris from the start, not as an afterthought.
